# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Arabic-speaking listeners who open the app for a short, calm listening session: resuming a recitation, starting a live radio stream, finding a reciter, or managing downloaded audio.

## Product Purpose

أثير القرآن makes Quran radio, recitations, listening history, and offline audio available through one fast, RTL-first web app. Success means a listener can begin or resume audio in one or two deliberate actions.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and reverent. The interface feels like a dependable listening instrument, never a luxury showcase or entertainment dashboard.

## Anti-references

- Gold-heavy "sacred luxury" dashboards, decorative geometric overlays, and glow-as-decoration.
- Dense metric tiles that compete with the listening task.
- Bouncy, persistent, or attention-seeking motion.

## Design Principles

1. Start or resume listening without searching for the next action.
2. Let hierarchy and spacing create calm; decoration is an exception.
3. Make the active player the one expressive material surface.
4. Preserve user context across browsing, playback, downloads, and offline use.
5. Use motion only to explain state and acknowledge intent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast targets, support keyboard and screen-reader operation, respect RTL and mixed Arabic/number content, and provide a complete reduced-motion path.
